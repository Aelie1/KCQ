Matsuko — Character Sheet v0

Role: Straightforward front-line attacker / tactical control
Identity: Matsuko always has something useful to do, even when partially restrained. Her physical and flame attacks cover different bondage vulnerabilities, while Compulsion lets her reshape dangerous enemy turns.

Ability	Type	Requirement	Effect
Punch	Attack	Arms	Reliable single-target physical damage. Basic fallback when Mouth skills are unavailable.
White Flame	Attack	Mouth	Reliable single-target flame damage. Primary ranged/magical offense.
Compulsion	Control / Support	Mouth	Choose one command. Shared cooldown, currently 2 turns.
Immolation	AoE Attack	Mouth	Deals damage to all enemies. Cooldown TBD.
Slave Brand	Limit Break	Arms	Severely cripples a normal enemy or applies a major encounter-long debuff to a boss.

Compulsion commands

Attack Me! — Retarget all compatible enemy intents to Matsuko for the round; Matsuko gains temporary Defense, current first-cut idea +2.
Stop! — Prevent or delay a compatible enemy action. Boss/major actions may be weakened rather than completely canceled.
Focus! — Give an ally a substantial temporary Hit/Defense buff.
Escape! — Give an ally a substantial temporary Escape/Assist bonus.

Using any command triggers the shared Compulsion cooldown.

Spirit Bond — Double Tech: Matsuko + Ko
Consumes both characters’ actions. White flame and black starlight combine for extremely high single-target damage.

Restraint profile

Arms skills: Punch, Slave Brand.
Mouth skills: White Flame, Compulsion, Immolation.
Losing one category narrows Matsuko considerably but does not shut her down.
Losing both leaves her badly compromised.
