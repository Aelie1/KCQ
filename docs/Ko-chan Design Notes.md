Ko-chan — Character Sheet v0

Role: Resilient magical attacker / reactive support
Identity: Ko continues functioning while increasingly restrained, but depends completely on her teammates to ever get free.

Ability	Type	Effect
Telekinesis	Attack	Reliable single-target damage.
Starlight Bindings	Attack / Control	Deals damage and binds the target, reducing its effectiveness. Current candidate: -Defense. Enhanced version may also reduce Hit.
Reflect	Support / Reaction	Prepares a reflection. The next compatible attack against Ko is negated and deals counter-damage to its attacker. Boss/scripted effects may only be partially reflectable.
Fairy Transformation	Self Buff	Temporarily grants +Hit / +Defense and empowers Ko's next eligible ability.
Power of Denial	Super	Deletes one eligible problem from reality: e.g. a restraint, non-boss enemy, hostile object/effect. Exact restrictions TBD.

Thousand Restraints Body — Passive
Ko can continue using Arms, Legs, and Mouth skills regardless of the corresponding bondage state. She cannot Escape herself. Her bondage still accumulates normally and counts toward capture and other effects.

Why Not? — Party Passive / Pre-Campaign
Choose one unlocked character-specific Why Not? modification before the campaign. It permanently breaks one normal rule/limitation for that character for the run.

Example: Hinari — Inventory clutter no longer prevents her from using Space abilities.

Spirit Bond — Double Tech: Ko + Matsuko
Consumes both characters' actions. Combines black starlight + white flame for enormous single-target damage.

Fairy Empowerment: Transformation gives one empowerment charge; Ko's next eligible ability consumes it for an enhanced version. Exact enhancements TBD.
